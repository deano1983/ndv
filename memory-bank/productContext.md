# Product Context

## Why this exists
NDV ("Neurodivergence") is a luxury shopping experience for neurodivergent women (18–55,
autism/ADHD/sensory-sensitive). Tagline direction: *"Luxury for the sensitive woman."*
Every product has a "second job: to soften something" (tagless seams, weighted blankets,
wireless bras, compression-as-comfort, tactile objects).

## Experience goals
- Hushed, tactile, sanctuary-like tone. Noir + champagne/rose palette (never pastel).
- Typography: Cormorant Garamond (display) + Inter (sans).
- Copy voice: quiet, precise, considerate. No urgency/spam patterns.

## Surfaces
- **Web** (TanStack Start SSR, Lovable-connected) — full experience incl. AI Concierge, MCP.
- **Native app** (Capacitor 8 shell around the SPA build) — catalog/cart/checkout via
  client-side Shopify Storefront API. Server-only features intentionally trimmed in v1.

## Catalog: "The Five Houses"
1. Atelier — ready-to-wear (cashmere, silk, tagless)
2. Intimates — soft-cup, wireless, sensory-friendly
3. Second Skin — seamless shapewear
4. Sanctuary Bedding — weighted blankets, silk sheets
5. Objects & Adornment — fine jewelry, tactile objects, calm-finish electronics
