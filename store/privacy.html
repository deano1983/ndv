You are sensitive; your data is treated the same way. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we will never do.

What we collect

If you browse only: nothing that identifies you. Pages are served without advertising trackers.

If you create an account: your email address and (optionally) your name, processed by Supabase (our authentication provider).

If you buy: checkout is completed on Shopify's secure checkout. Shopify processes your contact, shipping, and payment details as our commerce processor. We never see or store your full card number.

Your cart: items and quantities are kept in your device's local storage and in your Shopify cart so they follow you between visits.

What we never do

We do not sell your data. We do not run third-party advertising. We do not use cross-site tracking, fingerprinting, countdown-pressure analytics, or dark-pattern telemetry. There are no ad networks in this app.

Third-party processors

Shopify — catalog, cart, checkout, orders (shopify.com/legal/privacy). Supabase — authentication (supabase.com/privacy). Google Fonts — typeface delivery (receives your IP to serve fonts). Google Play / Apple App Store — their own install/crash diagnostics under their own policies.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA honored). Email [privacy@your-domain.com]; we respond within 30 days. Order records are retained only as long as tax and consumer law requires.

Security & retention

Traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS). Account data is kept until you delete your account; on-device cart data can be cleared anytime via app/browser storage.
