## Description (max 4000)
A quiet place to shop beautiful things.

NDV is designed for neurodivergent women who feel the world deeply. Every object
here has a second job: to soften something. We line the silk so the seam never
bites. We weight the blanket because the world is loud. We make the bra without
the wire, the shapewear without the squeeze.

THE FIVE HOUSES
Atelier — ready-to-wear in cashmere, silk, and tagless seams.
Intimates — luxury lingerie in sensory-friendly fabrics. Soft-cup, wireless.
Second Skin — seamless shapewear; compression as comfort, not constraint.
Sanctuary Bedding — weighted blankets, silk sheets, intimate bedding.
Objects & Adornment — fine jewelry, tactile objects, calm-finish electronics.

QUIET BY DESIGN
No countdown timers. No pop-ups. No noise. A hushed, tactile interface with
sensory notes on every product, plain-language sizing, and secure checkout
powered by Shopify.
